## Teaser videos

`assets/video/` holds the 75s teaser and three 15s clips shown in the hero
showcase, plus `flamyngo-tiktok.mp4` — a 24s vertical 9:16 cut for
TikTok / Reels (not embedded on the page). They are generated from `video-src/` (HTML motion graphics rendered
frame-by-frame with Playwright, plus a synthesised soundtrack) — run
`video-src/build.sh` to rebuild them after editing `video-src/film.html`.
